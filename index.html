GIF Aura - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application, "GIF Aura", handles your data and protects your privacy.

1. 100% Local Processing Guarantee

All GIF manipulations, video transcoding, and database operations are executed strictly on your local device. We do not operate any external cloud storage or servers. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Zero Network Transmissions

This application does not require an internet connection to function, nor does it establish any outbound network connections. Your files, assets, and creative works are 100% safe and private.

3. Permission Utilization Statement

We request access to certain features on your device solely to provide core functionality:

  • Gallery Access: Required to let you select existing photos and videos from your device for GIF creation.
  • Camera & Microphone Access: Requested strictly to support optional photo or video capturing directly within the app's media picker.

Because this application processes all media completely offline, all captured photos, videos, and audio are stored strictly on your local device. They are NEVER uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any external servers or third parties.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Since our application does not connect to the internet, any updates to this policy will only be reflected in new version updates of the app on the platform where you downloaded it.
